GradientOrbCard — the brand's signature atmospheric moment. A pastel radial bloom (mint/peach/lavender/sky/rose) drifts behind centered display copy. The orb is pure atmosphere — never a button fill or text color.

```jsx
<GradientOrbCard orb="mint" eyebrow="Creative" title="Lifelike speech, instantly" body="Generate natural voices in 32 languages." />
<GradientOrbCard orb="lavender" secondOrb="peach" title="Atmospheric by default" />
```

Props: `orb` (required feel), `secondOrb` (layered bloom), `eyebrow`, `title` (Newsreader 300 display), `body`. Pass `children` for a CTA. Keep one or at most two orbs — restraint is the point.
